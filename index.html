005 / Before you order

Important notes

These four points are what most problems come down to. Read them once and the whole thing goes smoothly.

1

Your account must not be verified

If your account is already verified or still inside an active subscription period, the gift cannot be applied. Wait for it to end, or cancel it first, then order. No refund applies in that case.

2

Not eligible? Try again in a few days

If the check says your account is not eligible yet, message us again after 1 to 3 days. Eligibility usually opens up on its own.

3

Do not change your profile during the process

Changing your username, display name, profile picture or cover photo while the order is being processed can make the account ineligible. Leave it as it is until the gift lands.

4

One 3-month gift per cycle

A 3-month subscription can only be taken once per cycle. You can subscribe again after the current one ends.

009 / FAQ

Questions, answered

How does the gift work?

We purchase a gift subscription and send it to the username you give us. It works the same for X Premium and Telegram Premium. You accept it from your own account, no password required, and it lands on your profile.

Do I need to share my password?

Never. A gift is sent to your username. We do not touch your login, your password or your account settings. You stay in full control the whole time.

Is my account eligible for a gift?

Your account must not be verified and your current subscription period must have expired. An account that is already verified, or still inside an active subscription period, cannot receive the gift. No refund applies in that case, so please check first with the eligibility tool at the top of this page.

Why is it cheaper than the official price?

We buy in bulk and pass the discount on. You pay our price, we gift the subscription to your account. Same official product, lower cost to you.

How fast is delivery?

Average delivery is 5 minutes to 24 hours, depending on the queue. You get a Telegram message the moment your gift is on its way.

How do I pay?

Everything about payment is handled privately in the Telegram chat. Message us, tell us which plan you want, and we will walk you through the available methods and the exact amount for that day. Nothing is published on this website.

Are 3 and 6-month plans official?

On X, official cycles are monthly and annual, so the 3 and 6-month options are our own reseller durations, delivered through the same gifting system. On Telegram the 3, 6 and 12-month options follow Telegram's own Premium gift durations.

Can I order more than one?

Yes. Message us with how many you need and the usernames for each one. Each unit is gifted to a separate account.
